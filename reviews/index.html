Plays by Google's rules and UK law

Google's review policy and the UK's Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 ban fake reviews, paying or rewarding people for reviews, and hiding negative ones. Mersey Stars never blocks anyone from Google, never writes reviews for customers, and never suggests offering incentives. Every customer, happy or not, can always reach your Google page. Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely free while we launch. Paid plans will come later with extra features. Founding businesses who join now keep a discount for life, and we'll always tell you well before anything changes.

What's a founding business?

One of the first Liverpool businesses to use Mersey Stars. You use it free during launch, tell us what works and what doesn't, and lock in a lifetime discount on any paid plan. Sign up with the form above. It takes a minute.

What do I need?

A Google Business Profile (it's free from Google and is what shows your business on Google Maps and Search). If you can see "Ask for reviews" when you search for your business while signed in, you're ready.

Where does the private feedback go?

Straight to your email or WhatsApp, whichever you choose during setup. We never see it.

Where is my customer list stored?

Only in the browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Settings to back it up, or to copy your setup to a second phone for your staff.

Can I offer a discount for a review?

No. Google doesn't allow rewards for reviews, and UK law treats incentivised reviews that aren't clearly disclosed as misleading. Just ask, and make asking easy. That's what this tool is for.

Does it work for businesses without a shop?

Yes. Trades and mobile businesses usually get the best results by sending a WhatsApp message the same day the job is finished.

Business details

Where do I find this?
  1. On your phone or computer, sign in to the Google account that manages your business.
  2. Search Google for your business name. Your Business Profile appears with your management tools.
  3. Tap Ask for reviews (sometimes under Read reviews → Get more reviews).
  4. Copy the link it gives you (it looks like g.page/r/…/review) and paste it above.

No access to the profile? Use Google's free Place ID finder: search your business and paste the ID that starts with "ChIJ".
