Privacy notice

Last updated 12 November 2026

In short: we only get the details you choose to send us. Your review page settings and customer list stay on your own device, and we never see them.

Who we are

Mersey Stars is run by Jonathan, based in Liverpool. For anything about your information, email jonathansmallbone889@gmail.com or call 07576 618414.

What we collect, and why

  • Founding business sign-ups. When you fill in the founding form and send it to us by WhatsApp or email, we receive what you entered: your business name, your name, phone, email, type of business, area, and your Google rating if you added it. We use it to contact you, set up Mersey Stars with you, and show you how your Google reviews change over time.
  • Messages you send us. If you call, WhatsApp or email us, we keep that conversation so we can help you.

We rely on your request to join (taking steps towards an agreement with you) and our legitimate interest in running and improving the service.

What stays on your device

Your business setup, your customer list and the messages you prepare are saved in your own browser. They aren't uploaded to us, so we never see them. You can back them up or delete them at any time in Settings.

Your customers

When a customer uses your review page, any private feedback goes straight from their phone to your email or WhatsApp. It doesn't pass through us. Google reviews are posted on Google, under Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We don't sell your information or share it for marketing. Messages travel through the service you chose to send them with (WhatsApp, your email provider or your phone network). This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical details such as IP addresses to keep the site running and secure.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The site uses your browser's own storage only to remember your setup on your device.

How long we keep it

We keep founding business details while we're working together, and for up to 12 months afterwards unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Just get in touch using the details above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
