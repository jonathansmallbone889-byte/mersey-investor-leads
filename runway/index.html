Runway

Know the week your cash runs short, before it happens.

Profitable businesses still fail because cash arrives late and bills don't wait. Enter what's in the bank, what's coming in and what's going out. Runway builds a 13-week forecast, flags the danger week, and gives you a ranked plan to get through it.

Import from your bank or accounting software

Add a bank statement export (the last 3 months works best) and/or your unpaid invoice list. Runway reads them in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and you review everything before it's added.

Where do I find these files?
  • Bank statement: in online banking, open your business account, choose the last 3 months of transactions and download or export as CSV. Every major UK bank, plus Monzo, Starling, Tide and Revolut, offers this.
  • Unpaid invoices: in Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent, Zoho or Sage, open your invoice list and export it as CSV. Include paid invoices too if you can, so Runway can learn how late each client pays.
