The rules changed on 1 May 2026

Are your rentals compliant with the Renters' Rights Act?

A free, plain-English checklist for Liverpool and Wirral landlords. Go through it property by property and see what you need to fix before it costs you a fine.

  • Section 21 "no-fault" evictions are gone
  • Fixed-term ASTs have become periodic tenancies
  • No more rental bidding, or asking for months of rent up front
  • Rent can only go up once a year, using a Section 13 notice
  • Tenants can ask to keep a pet, and you can't refuse without a good reason
  • Fines of up to £7,000, rising to £40,000 for repeat or serious breaches
